# Show Up: an app idea to get students off their phones and into the same room

Nov 16, 2026 · 12:53 a.m.

I spent tonight trying to work out what I could build for students at Trinity. Every campus idea I had, a library seat tracker, an events feed, a room-handover board, ran into something that already exists or a problem plenty of people are already working on. So I zoomed out and asked what students share everywhere. Loneliness keeps coming up: a global GWI survey found 80% of Gen Z had felt lonely in the past year, and a UK survey found one in four would turn to AI for company.
My idea is called Show Up. Once a day, everyone nearby gets the same notification: a 30-minute coffee in a public place with three to five other students. You sign up with a university email so everyone is verified, and afterwards you quietly mark who you'd meet again, which only counts if they say yes too. I haven't built anything yet. I'd start with a tiny website and do the matching by hand for two weeks at Trinity, then see whether people actually come back. If you're a student there, would you show up?
