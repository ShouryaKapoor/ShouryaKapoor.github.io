# Assignments are my life now ?
Nov 16 · 20:58 p.m.

So I just opened up my blackboard today and what scared me was not my marks but the no. of assignments I have due from today till 27th Nov. Bombarding students with assignments, oh yeah why not? Might as well just offer them a full time job on it. Do you honestly think that students can do all of that without the help of AI? 
And I am not just talking about the assignments where we can't use AI, I am talking about all of them. Even with AI, this isn't healthy.

On top of that they call us daily for some lectures that they think we will give a quick read when we get home but everyone knows that we won't. We won't look at them until the last reading week before the exam. I am happy about some things though that we don't have that much exams as I thought we would. That's one thing certainly overwhelming and different from India. But yeah one would not be able to study for 6 different exams as each of them have a syllabus that I can't even begin comprehend.

Also, I am thinking of making certain changes in the Vanepoint project on github, haven't really made a n announcement about it but you can check it out here : https://github.com/vanepoint-hq/vanepoint

Today was a day of tough challenges, hard decisions and bad weather. I mean how does it starts raining suddenly with high speed freezing winds. 

Had a meeting today related to group project, was somewhat fruitful and informative. Have an evaluation tomorrow but I don't know how I am gonna take the time out to study for it. Plus with all the cooking and all. Anyways will figure something out, I always do. The thing I wanna point out is that we are never gonna get rid of assignments in life, the only way we can get through them is to handle them timely before its too late. Anyways, here's to another day of surviving with a busy routine. Hoping we come out stronger tomorrow.
